Home / Download

Get the beta. Keep your data.

OASIS is free for Windows 10 and 11, with no account and no time limit. Download the installer from GitHub Releases, verify its checksum if you like, install, download one AI model — and you're done. The whole process takes about ten minutes.

Download for Windows Check requirements

Verify first

Trust, but checksum.

Every release ships with a SHA256 hash. On Windows, open a terminal and run:

certutil -hashfile OASIS.exe SHA256

Compare the output with the hash published on the release page. If it doesn't match, don't install it — and tell us in Issues.

After installing

Three steps to offline.

1

Open the app

First launch suggests a model matched to your hardware. Accept it or pick another.

2

Download the model

One download with pause and resume. This is the last thing that needs the internet.

3

Unplug and talk

Seriously: try it with the router off. Chat, dictate, ask it to remember something.

Stuck? The FAQ covers the usual suspects; Issues covers the rest — include your Windows version and model.
