What's new, newest first.

OASIS is in public beta (v0.2.0, September 2026). This page summarizes user-facing releases; the exhaustive technical history lives in the repo's CHANGELOG.

September 2026 · current

v0.2.0 — the Mono release.

  • Mono by default: the whole app now wears the monochrome Swiss scheme, light and dark, with verified contrast.
  • Voice that holds up: faster recognition startup, adaptive noise filtering, barge-in that doesn't eat your words.
  • Discord & Telegram bots with voice calls, per-server settings and speaker recognition (Max).
  • Android app + browser extension talking to your PC over the local network (Max).
  • Scheduled workflows in plain words, proactive notifications, code sandbox with retries.
  • Accessibility pass: keyboard-only use, screen-reader labels, honest loading and error states everywhere.

Earlier

v0.1.x — first public beta.

  • Local chat with streaming, model manager and Hugging Face downloads.
  • Spanish voice in and out, wake word included.
  • Memory + documents: semantic search over conversations, notes, PDF, Word and Excel.
  • 68 system tools with per-tool permissions and approval for risky actions.
