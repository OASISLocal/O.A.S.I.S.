This is where we'll post release notes, technical deep dives, and honest updates on where OASIS is headed — the same [roadmap]({{ '/changelog.html#roadmap' | relative_url }}) you see in the changelog, with the reasoning behind it.

A few things to expect here:

- **Release notes** every time a new version ships, in plain language, not just a changelog dump.
- **Deep dives** into how <span class="term" data-term="local-inference">local inference</span>, voice, and tool calling actually work under the hood.
- **Open build-in-public updates** — what's slow, what's hard, and what we're doing about it.

No account needed to read it, no tracking to measure it. If you want new posts delivered as they publish, subscribe to the [RSS feed]({{ '/blog/feed.xml' | relative_url }}) or watch [the repository](https://github.com/OASISLocal/O.A.S.I.S.) on GitHub.
