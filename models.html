Compatibility

GGUF is a format, not a promise.

  • What GGUF means: a single-file model format readable by the local runtime. It is the packaging, not a guarantee.
  • What is supported: compatible GGUF builds in the families the app lists — including DeepSeek, Phi and Mistral builds — subject to runtime and hardware.
  • What is not promised: that any GGUF file runs. Very large models, exotic architectures or unusual quantisations may not fit your RAM/VRAM or the runtime's support.
  • How you find out: the app inspects the file and warns you before downloading what your machine can realistically run.
