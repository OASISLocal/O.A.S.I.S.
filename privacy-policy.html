Privacy Policy.

Scope. This policy covers the O.A.S.I.S. website, Journal, public documentation, contact messages and Max waitlist. It does not describe the desktop application. App privacy documentation will be published separately before app distribution.

Looking for how the application handles your files, feature by feature? That is a different document with a different scope: the local-first data model. This page is the legal policy for the website itself.

This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit O.A.S.I.S. online, why it is processed, which providers may receive it and how to contact us. It is written for the public website, not as a promise that every product feature is network-independent.

1. Who is responsible?

The website is operated by the O.A.S.I.S. project. For privacy requests, contact hello@oasislocal.dev. If you need the legal identity or address of the responsible operator for a formal request, ask through that address and we will provide the applicable contact details.

3. Legal bases and use

  • Waitlist processing is based on your request and consent to receive the launch notification.
  • Replies to messages are processed to respond to you.
  • Security, abuse prevention and reliable delivery rely on the operator’s legitimate interest and provider obligations.
  • We do not use the website for behavioural advertising, profiling or sale of personal information.

4. Providers and external requests

The site is hosted and published through GitHub Pages. The homepage may request public repository data from the GitHub API to show a star count. The waitlist form sends its submission to an O.A.S.I.S.-configured Cloudflare Worker, which stores the waitlist record in Cloudflare KV. GitHub and Cloudflare may process technical data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Fonts and static assets used by normal page loads are served from this site. No Google Analytics, advertising pixels or third-party analytics SDK is installed.

5. Retention

  • Theme preference remains in your browser until you remove it or clear site data.
  • Waitlist records are retained until the relevant launch communication is completed or you request deletion, unless a longer period is required for security or legal obligations.
  • Email correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and maintain project records.
  • Hosting, CDN and provider logs follow the provider’s retention policies.

6. Your choices and rights

You can decline the waitlist, block optional requests, clear the theme key and contact us to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your waitlist data. You may also object where processing relies on legitimate interests. You can complain to the data protection authority in your country. We may need enough information to verify a request.

7. Children

The website is not directed at children. Do not submit another person’s information to the waitlist.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the website or its providers change. The effective date at the top indicates the current version. Material changes will be highlighted on the Journal or changelog where appropriate.

This website policy is informational and does not replace advice from a qualified privacy professional. See also the Website EULA and Third-party notices.
