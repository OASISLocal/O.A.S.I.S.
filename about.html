Home / About

Built by people who distrust the cloud.

OASIS is an independent project from Galicia, Spain, with one conviction: your computer should work for you, not report on you. No venture funding, no data business, no growth team — just a small team building the assistant we'd want on our own desks, and building it in the open.

Values

What we won't do.

  • No accounts, ever, for the core product. If a feature needs an account, it's a different product.
  • No telemetry. We don't know who you are, and we'd like to keep it that way — this site runs no trackers either.
  • No subscriptions. Max will be a single payment — charging rent on your own PC felt wrong.
  • No dark patterns. Permissions are explicit, risky actions ask first, uninstalling removes everything.
  • No hiding bugs. The changelog and blog say what's broken and what's next.

Contact

Talk to humans.

  • Questions & ideas: GitHub Discussions
  • Bug reports: GitHub Issues — Windows version + model + what happened.
  • Code: the repository, where the real roadmap lives.
